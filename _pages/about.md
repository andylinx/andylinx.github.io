## About

I'm a junior undergraduate in the JohnClass at [Zhiyuan College](https://en.zhiyuan.sjtu.edu.cn/), [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/). I am currently an undergraduate research intern, fortunate to be advised by Prof. [Chaowei Xiao](https://xiaocw11.github.io/index.html) at [Johns Hopkins University](https://ai.jhu.edu//). Previously, I had the honor of being mentored by Prof. [Yong-Lu Li](https://dirtyharrylyl.github.io/) in the [RHOS](https://mvig-rhos.com/) Lab at SJTU. I have previously interned at Tencent. My research interests include, but are not limited to, multimodal learning, as well as AI safety and security.

I'm always open to discussions and potential collaborations. Feel free to reach out if you'd like to connect!
